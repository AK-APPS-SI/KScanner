PRIVACY POLICY

Your pages.
On this device.

KScanner is a document scanner that keeps its library on the iPhone or iPad where you install it. This version does not require an account and does not sync documents to a KScanner server or through iCloud.

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Who we are

This Privacy Policy describes how the KScanner iOS app (“KScanner”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you use it. It also covers messages you choose to send to our support address. If you do not agree with this policy, do not use the app.

The short version

  • There is no KScanner account.
  • Scans, imports, recognized text, folders, transcripts, and search stay on the device where you installed the app.
  • This version does not upload that library to a developer-operated server, and it does not sync it with iCloud.
  • A file leaves the device when you share it, export a zip, fetch a link you pasted, email support, or when Apple speech services process audio because on-device recognition is unavailable.
  • We do not sell document contents, and the app does not show ads.

No account

KScanner does not ask for an email address, a password, or Sign in with Apple. There is no profile attached to your documents. Preferences you set in the app stay on the device.

Information stored on the device

Depending on what you capture, import, record, or type, the app may store:

  • Page images and the PDF or image files created from them
  • Documents created from photos, files shared into KScanner, links, QR content, or Speech to PDF transcripts
  • Document titles, types, folders, favorites, locks, and trash
  • Text recognized from a page, including dates, amounts, and other details the app suggests
  • Marks you add, such as notes, stamps, and a signature placed on a page
  • Hide-sensitive overlays you apply (blackout or pixelate). These conceal areas visually; the app may still retain the underlying page data needed to edit or undo
  • Optional audio you choose to keep with a Speech to PDF document
  • Recent search text, including text produced by optional voice search
  • Settings such as scan type, reading languages, appearance, haptics, and whether app lock is on

This information is used to provide scanning, import, reading, organization, search, sharing, and backup on that device. A password you set on an exported PDF is applied to that file. It is not stored in KScanner.

Where the library is stored

Documents and related files are stored locally on the iPhone or iPad where KScanner is installed, inside the app’s storage. This version does not use iCloud or CloudKit to synchronize or restore the library.

When you share content into KScanner from another app, a Share Extension may briefly hold that content in an App Group shared only with the KScanner install on the same device, so the main app can import it. That handoff stays on the device. It is not cloud sync.

Deleting KScanner, or erasing the device, deletes that local library unless you previously exported a zip and kept the file somewhere else. KScanner does not operate a server that receives your pages, recognized text, transcripts, or library contents.

If you export a zip and then save it in Files, iCloud Drive, or another app, that copy is under your control and under the terms of the place you saved it. That action is not library sync.

On-device processing

KScanner uses Apple frameworks on the device for camera capture, document detection, text recognition, search, and optional concealment suggestions such as faces or fields on a page. Suggestions such as a title or a document type are produced from the scan on the device. You can review and edit them.

KScanner does not send your page images or document text to a third-party cloud AI service for recognition.

Permissions

The app requests a system permission when you use the feature that needs it, not as a condition of opening KScanner. You can deny a permission and continue to use the rest of the app. You can change access later in iOS Settings.

  • Camera. Used to capture a page and to scan QR codes. Images and decoded content are stored in the app unless you export or share them.
  • Photo library. Used when you import an existing picture. The image you choose is copied into KScanner’s local storage.
  • Microphone and speech recognition. Used for Speech to PDF and optional voice search. KScanner does not upload audio to its own servers. Depending on language and device settings, recognition may use on-device processing or Apple speech services. When Apple processes audio, that handling follows Apple’s Privacy Policy. If you choose to keep audio with a Speech to PDF document, that recording stays in the local library until you remove it or delete the document or the app. Search text from voice search stays on the device until you clear recent searches or delete the app.
  • Face ID, Touch ID, or device passcode. Used if you turn on app lock, a lock on a document, or a lock on a folder. Biometric data is handled by iOS. KScanner does not receive or store a fingerprint or a face template. When app lock is on, the app switcher hides the pages. Locks are an authentication gate; they are not a separate encryption layer for document contents.

KScanner does not request location, contacts, or tracking permission. It does not send remote marketing notifications.

What leaves the device

Share creates a file only after you choose a format. The file is handed to the destination you pick in the system share sheet — Mail, Files, AirDrop, or another app. You are responsible for that copy.

Link to PDF fetches the URL you paste so the app can preview and save a personal copy. That request goes to the site or file host you named. KScanner does not operate that host. You are responsible for having the right to save and reproduce that content.

Export library, in Settings, creates a zip of the documents the app is keeping, including Trash. The zip leaves the app only when you save or send it. Importing a zip asks whether to replace the library on that device or add a duplicate.

If you email support, we receive the message you send, including any attachment. Do not include Apple Account passwords, PDF passwords, payment card numbers, or a full library zip unless you intend to share that file with us. We use support messages to answer the request, and we do not use them to advertise.

Information we collect

KScanner is designed not to collect your document contents on our servers. We do not sell that information. We do not use it for advertising. Apple’s privacy nutrition label for this app reports no tracking and no collected data types by the developer.

The current app does not include third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting software that receives your pages or recognized text. An optional App Store rating prompt, when shown, is provided by Apple.

Apple may process information related to downloading KScanner from the App Store under Apple’s Privacy Policy. We do not receive your Apple Account password or your payment card number from that process.

Purchases

This version of KScanner does not sell subscriptions or other in-app purchases. If a paid option is added later, this policy will be updated to describe what Apple shares with the app for that purchase, before the new practice applies.

Retention and deletion

Documents remain on the device until you delete them permanently from Trash, delete the app, or erase the device. Recent searches can be cleared in Settings without deleting documents.

Permanent deletion removes the copy kept by the app, including its pages and recognized text. It does not delete a file you already shared or a zip you already saved elsewhere. We do not claim that deletion erases every remnant the operating system or a backup of the whole device might still hold.

Support email is retained only as long as needed to handle the request and any follow-up, unless a longer period is required by law.

Children

KScanner is a general productivity tool. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly request contact information from children. If you believe a child has sent personal information to our support address, email us and we will delete that correspondence where we are able to do so.

Security

The library relies on the iOS app sandbox, on-device storage, and the protections of your device passcode. Optional app lock, folder lock, and per-document lock add a check when you return. No storage method is perfectly secure. Anyone who can unlock the device, or anyone who receives a zip or a shared file, can access the documents in that copy. Use a device passcode. Turn on app lock if the device is shared.

Hide sensitive conceals areas on a page for sharing or review. It is not a certified redaction workflow and should not be treated as permanently destroying the underlying information in every copy of the file.

Apple services

KScanner uses Apple system frameworks required for the features above, including the camera, Vision, VisionKit, Speech, LocalAuthentication, WebKit for webpage preview when you save a link, and the system share sheet. Apple’s handling of information through those services is governed by Apple’s Licensed Application End User License Agreement and Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Changes

We may update this policy when KScanner’s features or data practices change. The date at the top of this page will show the latest revision. If a future version adds iCloud sync, analytics, or another form of collection, we will update this policy before or when that practice applies.
