AFTER YOU SAVE

Organize, lock, and export on your terms.

▤

Organize

Keep documents in folders, mark favorites, and rename anything. Trash holds a file until you delete it for good.

✎

Markup

Add ink, stamps, highlights, notes, and a signature. Hide faces or fields when you share a sensitive page.

◉

Lock

⇪

Share

Send a PDF, Word, PowerPoint, Excel, JPEG, or text file. A PDF password is optional, and it is not saved in the app.

KEPT ON THIS DEVICE

No account.
No KScanner cloud.

Scanning, reading, and search run on the device. This version does not sync a library with iCloud. A copy leaves the device when you share a file, export a zip, fetch a link you pasted, or use Apple speech services when on-device recognition is unavailable.
