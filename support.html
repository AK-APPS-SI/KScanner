01 · GETTING STARTED

Scan the first page

KScanner opens ready to work. You do not create an account. On the Scan tab, Start camera finds the page, and you can check it before it is saved.

Add a document

  1. Open Scan. Auto is the usual starting point, or pick a type such as Receipt or ID.
  2. Tap Start camera. Capture one page, or keep adding pages to the same document.
  3. Review the crop. Rotate a page if the orientation is wrong.
  4. Save. KScanner suggests a title from the scan and the date. Rename it whenever you like.
Tip Even light and a page that fills the frame give the camera a cleaner edge to follow.
02 · MORE WAYS IN

Import, Link, QR, Speech, and Share

Beside the camera, the Scan hub offers Import, Link to PDF, QR to PDF, and Speech to PDF. You can also share into KScanner from another app.

Import

Choose photos from the library or files from Files. They become a PDF in your library. You can keep several images as one document or create separate documents, depending on the options shown.

Link to PDF

Paste an http or https webpage or file URL. Preview the page, then save your own PDF copy. The saved file is a personal copy — not an official document issued by that website. You must have the right to save and reproduce the content.

QR to PDF

Point the camera at a QR code. KScanner reads what is inside and lets you save it as a PDF in the library.

Speech to PDF

Record a quick note, meeting, or free recording, or import an audio file. Review the transcript, optionally show times, then save a PDF. You may keep the audio with the document. The first time you record, iOS asks for the microphone and speech recognition.

Speech privacy KScanner does not upload audio to its own servers. Depending on language and device, recognition may use on-device processing or Apple speech services.

Share from another app

In Photos, Safari, Files, or another app, open Share and choose KScanner for supported images, files, text, or a URL. Open In also works for PDFs, images, and audio. Audio can open into Speech to PDF.

03 · READING

Text stays on the device

After a scan or import, KScanner reads the page on the device. That text is what search uses, and what a text export contains. You can correct a title or the document type if the suggestion is off.

Reading follows the languages the device already uses. In Settings → Reading you can narrow that to specific languages when a stack of pages is in one language, or turn Automatic back on.

If a line is missed Small type, glare, and handwriting are harder to read. Retake the page, or type the title yourself. Recognition assists entry. It does not certify the text.
04 · ORGANIZE & MARKUP

Folders, favorites, hide, and sign

File a document in a folder, mark it as a favorite, or leave it in the main library. Deleting moves it to Trash. Trash keeps it until you delete it permanently.

On a page you can adjust filter, crop, and rotate; hide faces or sensitive fields; and mark with ink, stamps, highlight, notes, or a signature. A signature is a mark on the page, not a certified electronic-signature service. Hide sensitive conceals areas for sharing — it is not irreversible certified redaction.

05 · SEARCH

Find the page, not just the file name

Search looks through the text read from your documents. Open a result and KScanner can take you to the page that matched. Recent searches stay on the device until you clear them in Settings → Library.

Voice search is optional and separate from Speech to PDF. The first time you use it, iOS asks for speech recognition and the microphone.

06 · SHARE

A file is created when you ask for one

Share can produce a PDF, a Word file, a PowerPoint file, an Excel file, JPEG pages, or plain text. Nothing is copied to another app until you pick a destination in the share sheet.

PDF quality is set in Settings → Scanning: High, Balanced, or Compact. High stays closest to the scan. Compact makes a smaller file.

You may protect a PDF with a password. Anyone who opens that PDF needs the password. KScanner does not save the password in the app. If you forget it, the protected file cannot be opened from that password later.

Text export A text file is available after the page has been read. If nothing has been read yet, KScanner will say so.
07 · BACKUP

Keep a copy you control

This version stores the library on the device. It does not sync documents through iCloud. The way to move a library is a zip you export yourself.

Before you switch devices or delete the app

  • Open Settings and export the library. The zip includes documents in Trash.
  • Save that zip somewhere you control — Files, another device, or a drive you manage. Putting the zip in iCloud Drive is your choice. It is not automatic sync.
  • On the new install, import the zip. You can replace the library on that device, or add the zip as a duplicate and keep what is already there.
Important Deleting KScanner deletes the library stored by the app. Import a zip you already saved if you want those documents back.
08 · LOCK

Cover the pages when you leave

Settings → Lock can require Face ID, Touch ID, or your device passcode when you return to KScanner. While that lock is on, the app switcher hides the pages. Set a passcode on the device before you turn the lock on.

A lock on one document or one folder is separate. It stays on that item inside the library.
